MY DEFAULT SETTINGS
01 Find the problem beneath the request.+

A ticket describes what broke. A product question asks who it affects, how often it happens, and what solving it would change.

02 Make the trade-off visible.+

A prototype helps me make an idea concrete. Comparing alternatives makes the reasoning behind it concrete too.

03 Measure value beyond the launch.+

A working demo is a starting point. Adoption, quality, trust, and sustained user value are the next questions.
